We Are Committed

Our Mission Statement

The mission of Brown Behavioral Health Services is to provide proficient and competent care related to diagnosing and treating psychiatric illnesses in pediatrics and adults while improving overall health status. BBHS is committed to the mental health population and is eager to serve each individual holistically while enhancing mental health awareness, promoting individual empowerment, and increasing access to treatment and services for persons living with mental illnesses.

Send Us a Message

Share your thoughts, questions, and concerns with us by leaving us a message.

Please do not include private medical details in this form. For medical concerns, call 731-228-9068 or use the patient portal.

If you have an emergency or need immediate assistance, please call 911. The Tennessee Statewide Crisis Line, available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, is a free resource for anyone experiencing a mental health crisis. All calls are routed to a trained crisis counselor in your area, who will provide support and guidance and work to connect you with appropriate community supports.

TN Crisis Line: 1-855-CRISIS-1 (1-855-274-7471)988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (formerly 1-800-273-TALK)
