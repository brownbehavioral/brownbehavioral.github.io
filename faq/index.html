Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about appointments, insurance, billing, and your care.

Who can be seen at BBHS?

We welcome children, adolescents, adults, and older adults ages 5 and up. We serve patients throughout Tennessee and Mississippi in our Bolivar and Olive Branch offices, on our Mobile Health Clinic, and by secure telehealth.

How do I schedule my first appointment?

Book online anytime through healow and choose New Patient, use the healow app with practice code ACGHDD, or call 731-228-9068.

What are your office hours?

Our offices are open Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Extended evening hours and Saturday appointments are available based on each provider’s schedule.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Secure video visits are available for patients located in Tennessee or Mississippi at the time of the visit. You will need a private, quiet space and a phone, tablet, or computer with a camera and internet connection. Our team sends your video visit instructions before your appointment.

What insurance do you accept?

We accept Medicare, TennCare, Mississippi Medicaid, and most major commercial and Marketplace plans. See Insurance Accepted for the full list, explained in plain language, or call us to verify your coverage.

What if I do not have insurance?

No one will be denied access to services due to inability to pay. A discounted/sliding fee schedule is available based on family size and income. Free care for uninsured patients is offered through available and limited Healthcare Resiliency Program (HRP) grant funds. Uninsured and self-pay patients also have the right to a Good Faith Estimate of expected charges.

How do I pay my bill?

Pay your copay or balance online with Scratchpay, finance care with CareCredit, or call 731-228-9068 to pay by phone. See Payment Options.

What should I bring to my first visit?

A photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, a list of your current medications and doses, and any prior records, evaluations, or school reports that may help your provider. If you receive an eClinicalWorks check-in link by text or email, please complete it before your visit.

How do I request a medication refill?

Send a refill request through the patient portal or the healow app, or call 731-228-9068. Please allow time for your provider to review the request, and do not wait until you are out of medication.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Call 731-228-9068 as soon as you know you need to change your appointment so we can offer the time to another patient. You can also manage appointments in the healow app.

Do you offer services in Spanish?

Yes. Interpreter services are available free of charge. If you speak Spanish, our team can assist you, and a free interpreter can be provided for any language. ATENCIÓN: si habla español, tiene a su disposición servicios gratuitos de asistencia lingüística. Llame al 731-228-9068.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. We protect your health information under HIPAA, and records of substance use disorder treatment, including our MAT program, have additional protection under 42 C.F.R. Part 2. Read our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Do you see University of Memphis students?

Yes. BBHS providers see University of Memphis students at the Student Health and Counseling Center, 214 Wilder Tower, on Fridays from 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., in person or by telehealth. Students schedule by calling 901-678-2068.

How do providers refer a patient?

Use our referral form or fax records to 901-425-9773. For urgent referrals, please also call 731-228-9068.

What should I do in a crisis?

If you are in danger, call 911. For a mental health or substance use crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline), call the Tennessee Statewide Crisis Line at 855-274-7471, or call the Mississippi Department of Mental Health Helpline at 1-877-210-8513. Help is free and available 24/7.

Still have a question? Call 731-228-9068 or contact us.
