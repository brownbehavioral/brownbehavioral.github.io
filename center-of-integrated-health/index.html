Our Nonprofit Partner

Center of Integrated Health

Center of Integrated Health is a Tennessee nonprofit and 501(c)(3) public charity founded by Dr. Avanti Cross-Brown to extend health, wellness, and opportunity to rural and underserved families beyond the walls of the clinic.

Working hand in hand with Brown Behavioral Health Services, the Center pursues grants, community partnerships, and charitable support for programs that strengthen youth, families, and communities across West Tennessee and North Mississippi.

  • 501(c)(3) Public Charity
  • Founded 2023
  • Bolivar, Tennessee
What We Do

Programs and Services

Youth Sports and Wellness

A youth sports program serving Hardeman County students for five years, now equipped through a Good Sports in-kind award so cost is never a barrier to playing.

Youth Mental Health

Wellness education and mental health support for young people, coordinated with the clinical care of Brown Behavioral Health Services.

Community Health Partnerships

Partnerships with schools, churches, and community organizations that bring outreach, resources, and services to rural families.
