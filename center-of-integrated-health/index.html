Our Nonprofit Partner

Center of Integrated Health

Center of Integrated Health is a Tennessee nonprofit and 501(c)(3) public charity founded by Dr. Avanti Cross-Brown to extend health, wellness, and opportunity to rural and underserved families beyond the walls of the clinic.

Working hand in hand with Brown Behavioral Health Services, the Center pursues grants, community partnerships, and charitable support for programs that strengthen youth, families, and communities across West Tennessee and North Mississippi.

  • 501(c)(3) Public Charity
  • Founded 2023
  • Bolivar, Tennessee

Founder and Executive Director: Dr. Avanti Cross-Brown, APRN, DNP, PMHNP-BC, CARN-AP, NRCME

What We Do

Programs and Services

Delivered in partnership with Brown Behavioral Health Services.

Youth Sports and Wellness

A youth sports program serving Hardeman County students for five years, equipped through a Good Sports in-kind award so cost is never a barrier to playing.

Youth Mental Health

Wellness education and mental health support for young people, coordinated with Brown Behavioral Health Services.

Free Care for Uninsured Patients

Helping uninsured neighbors access behavioral health and primary care through Brown Behavioral Health Services.

Mobile Health Outreach

Bringing screenings, education, and care to rural communities through the Brown Behavioral Health Services Mobile Health Clinic.

School-Based Health and Telehealth

Supporting school health programs and school-based telehealth so students can get care without leaving campus.

Community Health Fairs and Screenings

Free health fairs and mental health screenings that connect families with care, benefits, and local resources.

Food Access and Nutrition

Community food access, gardens, and nutrition education that support chronic disease prevention.

Substance Use Recovery Support

Recovery resources, education, and peer support that complement addiction treatment and help people stay well.

Reentry and Correctional Health Support

Supporting people returning home from incarceration with connections to health care, recovery, and community resources.

Get Involved

Partner, Volunteer, or Give

We welcome partnerships with funders, schools, faith communities, and local organizations who share our commitment to healthier rural communities. Center of Integrated Health is a 501(c)(3) public charity, and contributions may be tax-deductible as allowed by law.
