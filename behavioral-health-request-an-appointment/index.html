Request an Appointment

Book online through healow, in the healow app, or by phone.

Online Scheduling with healow

Book your next appointment online.

No need to wait for our office to open the next day. BBHS uses eClinicalWorks, and appointments are booked online through healow, eClinicalWorks’ secure scheduling and patient app.

Book Now on healow Call 731-228-9068

How to book on healow

  1. Click Book Now on healow. Our practice page on healow.com opens in a new window.
  2. Choose your provider and visit reason. Select New Patient if this is your first visit with BBHS, or Established Patient if you have been seen before.
  3. Pick an available date and time, in office or by telehealth.
  4. Enter your name, date of birth, phone, email, and insurance. healow will send a verification code by text or email to confirm it is you.
  5. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Extended evening hours and Saturday appointments are available based on each provider’s schedule. Online booking is open 24/7.
  6. Watch for your confirmation by text or email. Before your visit you may also receive an eClinicalWorks check-in link to complete your forms, insurance, and consents from your phone.

Prefer the App?

Book in the healow App

  • Download the free healow™ app from the App Store or Google Play.
  • Search for our practice using practice code ACGHDD.
  • Tap Book Appointment, then choose your provider, visit reason, and time.
  • Telehealth visits: our team will send your secure video visit instructions before your appointment.
New Patients

New to BBHS?

We are welcoming new patients ages 5 and up at our Bolivar and Olive Branch offices and by secure telehealth. Book your first visit on healow in minutes and choose New Patient as your visit type, or call 731-228-9068 and our team will schedule you and set up your eClinicalWorks patient portal.
