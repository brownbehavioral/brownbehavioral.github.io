About Us

Mental health, minor medical, weight loss, substance abuse treatment, and IV hydration services across Tennessee and Mississippi.

Who We Are

Accessible care for rural communities

Brown Behavioral Health Services, PLLC takes pride in offering mental health, minor medical, weight loss, substance abuse treatment, and IV hydration services across the state of Tennessee and Mississippi.

Our in person and telehealth capabilities provide a more accessible approach to healthcare across rural communities. We encourage you to choose a premiere clinic such as ours to help facilitate your mental health needs as well as other medical services.

Our Mission

The mission of Brown Behavioral Health Services is to provide proficient and competent care related to diagnosing and treating psychiatric illnesses in pediatrics and adults while improving overall health status. BBHS is committed to the mental health population and is eager to serve each individual holistically while enhancing mental health awareness, promoting individual empowerment, and increasing access to treatment and services for persons living with mental illnesses.

Our Vision

“To provide client-centered, expert mental health services such as evaluations, patient advocacy, and medication management to the medically underserved communities with an aim to treat conditions such as Schizophrenia, Bipolar Disorders, ADHD, Depression, Anxiety, Substance Abuse, and many more.”

Dr. Avanti Cross-Brown, DNP, PMHNP-BC
