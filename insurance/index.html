Plain-Language Guide

Not Sure What Your Plan Is?

Look at the front of your insurance card. In Tennessee, Medicaid is called TennCare. In Mississippi, Medicaid plans are often called MississippiCAN. If you have both Medicare and Medicaid, you may be in a D-SNP or BlueCare Plus plan. Tap a group to learn more.

No insurance? No one will be denied access to services due to inability to pay. A sliding fee schedule, CareCredit, and Scratchpay are available. Payment options →

Self-pay programs (DOT physicals, Slim Program, DUI School, and MS medical card certification) are not billed to insurance.

Commercial & Marketplace: what these plans are

These are plans you get through a job, buy on your own, or purchase through the Health Insurance Marketplace (HealthCare.gov).

  • UnitedHealthcare: Employer, individual, and Medicare Advantage plans from UnitedHealth Group, including UMR and Optum behavioral health plans.
  • BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee: Employer and individual plans for Tennessee residents.
  • BlueCross BlueShield of Mississippi: Employer and individual plans for Mississippi residents.
  • BlueCard (Out-of-State Blue Plans): BlueCross BlueShield plans from other states, accepted through the national BlueCard program. Look for the suitcase logo on your card.
  • BCBS Federal Employee Program: BlueCross BlueShield coverage for federal employees, retirees, and their families.
  • Aetna: Employer, individual, and Medicare Advantage plans from CVS Health.
  • Cigna: Employer and individual plans.
  • Ambetter: Individual and family plans sold on the Health Insurance Marketplace.
  • Oscar: Individual and family plans sold on the Health Insurance Marketplace.
  • Alliant Health Plans: Individual, family, and employer plans.
Medicare & Medicare Advantage: what these plans are

Medicare is federal health insurance for people 65 and older and for some younger people with disabilities. Medicare Advantage plans are Medicare plans offered by private companies.

  • Original Medicare: The red, white, and blue Medicare card from the federal government. Covers people 65 and older and some people with disabilities.
  • Railroad Medicare: Medicare for retired railroad workers and their families. Your card will say “Railroad Retirement Board.”
  • Dual Special Needs Plans (D-SNP): Medicare Advantage plans for people who have both Medicare and Medicaid (TennCare or Mississippi Medicaid).
  • BlueCare Plus: A BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee plan for people who have both Medicare and TennCare.
  • BlueAdvantage: BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee’s Medicare Advantage plan.
  • Wellcare: Medicare Advantage and Medicare prescription plans offered by Centene.
  • Cigna Healthspring: Cigna’s Medicare Advantage plans.
  • Humana: Medicare Advantage plans, plus employer and individual plans.
  • Devoted Health: A Medicare Advantage plan for people with Medicare.
TennCare (TN Medicaid): what these plans are

TennCare is Tennessee’s Medicaid program for eligible children, families, pregnant women, and adults. TennCare members choose or are assigned a health plan, and the plan name appears on your card.

  • BlueCare Tennessee: A TennCare health plan offered by BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee.
  • TennCare Select: A TennCare plan mainly for children in state custody and some children with special health care needs.
  • Wellpoint: A TennCare health plan, formerly called Amerigroup. Wellpoint also offers Medicare and other plans.
  • UnitedHealthcare Community Plan: A TennCare health plan offered by UnitedHealthcare.
Mississippi Medicaid: what these plans are

Mississippi Medicaid covers eligible children, families, pregnant women, and adults. Many members are enrolled in a MississippiCAN or CHIP health plan, and the plan name appears on your card.

  • Magnolia Health: A Mississippi Medicaid (MississippiCAN and CHIP) health plan.
  • Molina Healthcare: A Mississippi Medicaid (MississippiCAN and CHIP) health plan. Molina also offers Marketplace plans.
  • TrueCare: A Mississippi Medicaid (MississippiCAN) health plan.
Other Plans: what these plans are

We also work with these plans and behavioral health networks.

  • TRICARE: Health coverage for active-duty service members, retirees, and their families.
  • Anthem: Employer and individual plans.
  • Carelon Behavioral Health: A behavioral health network that manages mental health benefits for some plans.
  • Magellan: A behavioral health network that manages mental health benefits for some plans.
