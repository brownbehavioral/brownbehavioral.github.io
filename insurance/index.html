Insurance Accepted

Medicare, TennCare, Mississippi Medicaid, and major private plans, explained in plain language.

Not Sure What Plan You Have?

We Will Help You Figure It Out

  • Look at the front of your insurance card. The company name and plan name are usually printed at the top.
  • In Tennessee, Medicaid is called TennCare. Your card will show BlueCare, TennCare Select, or Wellpoint.
  • In Mississippi, Medicaid plans are often called MississippiCAN. Your card will show Magnolia, Molina, or TrueCare.
  • Have both Medicare and Medicaid? You may be in a D-SNP or BlueCare Plus plan. We accept these.

Call us and we will check your coverage before your visit.

Call 731-228-9068

Bring your insurance card and a photo ID to every visit.

Medicare

Medicare and Medicare Advantage

Medicare is federal health insurance for people 65 and older and for some younger people with disabilities. Medicare Advantage plans are Medicare plans offered by private companies.

Original Medicare

The red, white, and blue Medicare card from the federal government. Covers people 65 and older and some people with disabilities.

Medicare Part B

Railroad Medicare

Medicare for retired railroad workers and their families. Your card will say “Railroad Retirement Board.”

RRB Medicare

Dual Special Needs Plans (D-SNP)

Medicare Advantage plans for people who have both Medicare and Medicaid (TennCare or Mississippi Medicaid).

Tennessee Medicaid

TennCare

TennCare is Tennessee’s Medicaid program for eligible children, families, pregnant women, and adults. TennCare members choose or are assigned a health plan, and the plan name appears on your card.

BlueCare Tennessee

A TennCare health plan offered by BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee.

BlueCare

A TennCare plan mainly for children in state custody and some children with special health care needs.

Offered by BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee

Mississippi Medicaid covers eligible children, families, pregnant women, and adults. Many members are enrolled in a MississippiCAN or CHIP health plan, and the plan name appears on your card.

Good to Know

Is my plan accepted for every service?

Coverage can vary by plan, provider, and service. Some self-pay programs, such as DOT physicals, the Slim Program, DUI School, and Mississippi medical cannabis certification, are not billed to insurance. Call 731-228-9068 and we will confirm your benefits before your visit.
